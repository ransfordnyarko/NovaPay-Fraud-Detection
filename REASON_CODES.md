# Reason Code Framework

This document defines the standardized reason codes used to explain
fraud predictions generated by the model.

Reason codes represent **high-level fraud dimensions** rather than
individual model features. Each code may act as either a
risk-increasing or risk-reducing signal depending on transaction context.

---

## RC01 – Destination Currency / Corridor Risk

**Description:**  
Risk associated with the destination currency or payment corridor.

**Example signals:**

- Destination currency (e.g. MXN, PHP)
- Corridor-level risk indicators

---

## RC02 – Source Currency Risk

**Description:**  
Risk related to the source currency of funds.

**Example signals:**

- Source currency (e.g. USD, GBP)

---

## RC03 – Home Country Risk Profile

**Description:**  
Risk associated with the account’s home country or jurisdiction.

**Example signals:**

- Account home country
- Cleaned or normalized country indicators

---

## RC04 – Location / Geographic Inconsistency

**Description:**  
Mismatch between expected and observed transaction location signals.

**Example signals:**

- Location mismatch flags
- IP country vs account country inconsistency

---

## RC05 – Device / IP Trust Profile

**Description:**  
Trustworthiness of the device and network used to perform the transaction.

**Example signals:**

- New or unfamiliar device
- IP address reputation
- Device or IP risk buckets
- Device trust score

---

## RC06 – Account Tenure and Transaction Behavior Pattern

**Description:**  
Behavioral and temporal patterns associated with the account.

**Example signals:**

- Transaction amount patterns
- Velocity indicators
- Account age or tenure
- Time-of-day or day-of-week behavior
- Historical chargeback activity

---

## Design Notes

- Only six reason codes are used to balance interpretability and coverage.
- Raw model features are aggregated into these concepts for explainability.
- Direction (risk vs protection) is reported per transaction, not encoded
  in the reason code itself.
